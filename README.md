# Software Engineering Lab – Section G

This repository contains the **student handout, scenario assignments, and problem statements** for the Software Engineering Lab.

## How to Use This Repository

Please follow the steps below before starting your lab work.

### 1. Refer to the Student Handout

First, go through the **Lab1_RE_student_handout.docx** provided in this repository.

The handout contains the lab instructions, requirements, deliverables, and steps you need to follow for the respective lab.

### 2. Check Your Assigned Scenario

Check the **sec_g_scenarios_assigned.xlsx sheet** provided for your section to find the scenario assigned to you.

Your assigned scenario will be based on your class/section allocation.

### 3. Download Your Problem Statement

Once you identify your assigned scenario, go to the **Scenarios for Lab 1–3** folder.

Find your assigned scenario and download/open the corresponding **problem statement**.

### 4. Complete the Lab

Use the problem statement together with the instructions in the student handout to complete the required lab activities and deliverables.

Make sure you follow all the requirements specified in the handout.

## Important

* Check the **Excel sheet first** to identify your assigned scenario.
* Use only the **problem statement corresponding to your assigned scenario**.
* Refer to the **student handout** for instructions and required deliverables.
* Do not use a different scenario unless instructed by the faculty/TA.

Good luck with your lab!
